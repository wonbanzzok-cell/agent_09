# Card News Plan: Essential Checklist for First-Time Campers (v1)

## Overview

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Topic | Camping |
| Title | "Your first camping trip? Just pack these." A must-have checklist for beginners |
| Channel | Instagram card news (1:1 or 4:5) |
| Number of cards | 8 (card 1 is the thumbnail) |
| Target audience | People in their 20s and 30s going camping for the first time, and families |
| Goal | A checklist that readers save and pull up while packing |
| Key message | Sort your gear into four groups (shelter, sleep, living, cooking) and nothing gets left behind |
| Tone | Friendly and easy, with short sentences and checkbox-style lists |

## Background

- Korea's camping market is estimated at over 10 trillion won, with 6 to 7 million campers (2025 estimates).
- Most campers go with family (72.4%), and regular tents are the most common way to stay (58.0%) (2021 Camping User Survey).
- Splitting gear into shelter, sleep, living, and cooking makes packing easier.
- Common beginner mistakes include skipping the rainfly on clear nights (the sleeping bag gets wet from condensation) and forgetting warm clothes for chilly nights.

## Design direction

- Colors: forest green (main), warm beige (background), sunset orange (accent)
- Fonts: bold rounded sans-serif for titles, plain sans-serif for body text
- Visual style: flat illustrations of gear, with a checkbox icon (☑) in front of every item
- Layout: card number at the top right (e.g. 2/8), and the same series label at the bottom of every card ("Camping Starter Kit")
- One idea per card, five checklist items at most

## Card-by-card plan

### Card 1. Thumbnail

- **Main copy:** Your first camping trip? Just pack these!
- **Sub copy:** A must-have checklist for beginners ✅
- **Image concept:** Camping gear laid out neatly on a picnic mat, seen from above (flat lay). A small tent and a lantern in the center.
- **Design notes:** Put the main copy in large type on the upper third. Add a small "Save it" badge in a corner.

### Card 2. Intro: Sort your gear into four groups

- **Title:** Sort gear into 4 groups and you won't forget anything
- **Body:**
  - 🏕️ Shelter: your house for the night
  - 🛏️ Sleep: a warm night's rest
  - 🪑 Living: comfort at the site
  - 🍳 Cooking: the best part of camping
- **Visual:** Four icons in a 2×2 grid
- **Design notes:** Use the same four icons as section labels on cards 3 to 6.

### Card 3. Shelter

- **Title:** 🏕️ Shelter: your house for the night
- **Checklist:**
  - ☑ Tent (check how many people it fits)
  - ☑ Rainfly
  - ☑ Footprint (ground sheet)
  - ☑ Pegs and a hammer
  - ☑ Tarp (shade from sun and rain)
- **Tip box:** Put the rainfly on even if the forecast is clear. Condensation at night can soak your sleeping bag.
- **Visual:** A tent with each part labeled

### Card 4. Sleep

- **Title:** 🛏️ Sleep: a warm night's rest
- **Checklist:**
  - ☑ Sleeping bag (check its temperature rating)
  - ☑ Mat or air mattress
  - ☑ Pillow
  - ☑ Extra blanket
- **Tip box:** The cold comes up from the ground. A thick mat matters more than a thick sleeping bag.
- **Visual:** A layered diagram of ground → mat → sleeping bag

### Card 5. Living

- **Title:** 🪑 Living: comfort at the site
- **Checklist:**
  - ☑ Chairs and a table
  - ☑ Lantern (one main light and one small light)
  - ☑ Power bank or portable battery
  - ☑ Extension cord (for sites with power)
  - ☑ Warm clothes and spare clothes
- **Tip box:** Nights get cold even in summer. Always pack a long-sleeved layer.
- **Visual:** A camping chair and table with a lit lantern

### Card 6. Cooking

- **Title:** 🍳 Cooking: the best part of camping
- **Checklist:**
  - ☑ Burner and gas canisters
  - ☑ Cookware (pot and frying pan)
  - ☑ Cooler and ice packs
  - ☑ Dish soap and a sponge
  - ☑ Trash bags (sort recyclables)
- **Tip box:** Wash dishes only at the sink area, and take your trash home with you.
- **Visual:** A camp burner and cookware set

### Card 7. Easy-to-forget items

- **Title:** ⚠️ The things everyone forgets
- **Checklist:**
  - ☑ First aid kit and personal medicine
  - ☑ Insect repellent and sunscreen
  - ☑ Carbon monoxide alarm
  - ☑ Rain gear
  - ☑ Gloves (for setting up and handling fire)
- **Tip box:** Never use a heater or brazier inside a closed tent. Bring a carbon monoxide alarm.
- **Visual:** An open pouch with these small items spilling out

### Card 8. Closing and call to action

- **Title:** You're all set for your first camping trip! 🏕️
- **Body:**
  - Shelter, sleep, living, cooking, plus easy-to-forget items
  - Check the weather forecast before you leave
- **Call to action:** 📌 Save this and use it while you pack / 👥 Share it with a friend you're camping with
- **Visual:** A small summary of all five checklists in one image
- **Design notes:** Put save and share icons at the bottom, and add the series label for the next post ("Coming next: 5 mistakes beginners make").
