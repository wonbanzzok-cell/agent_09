# LEGODT (레고트): Korean customizable tumbler and lifestyle brand

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Scope: Brand overview, products, prices, customization, sales channels in Korea and Japan, pop-up history (2024 to October 2026), collaborations, social media, and suggested content for a brand introduction web page. Not covered: company financials for LEGODT alone (not published), follower counts, and full current price lists (official stores could not be loaded). Not to be confused with LEGO or the Hiroshima University club "Leggodt".

## Summary

- LEGODT is a contemporary lifestyle brand from the Korean ceramics and tableware brand Odense (오덴세). Odense is run by Brandworks Korea (브랜드웍스코리아), a CJ ENM subsidiary [1][4][5][10]. The "launched by Odense" claim is **confirmed**.
- The name started as a colorful, modular tableware line inside Odense, on sale by February 2022. It grew into a tumbler-led lifestyle brand from 2023, with the Loop Tumbler listed from September 2023 [3][5][6][9].
- The core idea is **TUMGGU (텀꾸)**, a word made from "tumbler" (텀블러) and "to decorate" (꾸미다). Buyers dress up a Loop Tumbler with charms, keyrings, stickers, straps, and colored sleeves. In 2026 the brand added a modular tumbler built from 28 parts (handle, lid, body, sleeve) [11][15][21][25].
- It sells through its own online stores, Odense stores, 29CM, Musinsa, and Olive Young in Korea. In Japan it sells through legodt.jp, run by Manamana Co., Ltd., and through pop-ups at Hankyu Umeda, Shibuya Modi, Grand Tree Musashi-Kosugi, and PLAZA at Tamagawa Takashimaya S.C. [1][11][16][21][22][23][25][27].
- The tone is playful, colorful, and pop, aimed at young (MZ) shoppers. Collaborations include Pokémon, Disney's Zootopia, Mangom (망그러진 곰) through Olive Young, PLUPPY, and wiggle wiggle [13][16][18][28][29][30].

## Background

- **Parent brand.** Odense is a Korean tableware brand named after the Danish city of Odense. It is known for plating-focused dishes and as a wedding-gift brand [3]. In December 2022, CJ ENM moved Odense's business into its new brand subsidiary, Brandworks Korea, which started on January 2, 2023. Odense had 38 department-store locations at that time [4]. The Odense online store lists (주)브랜드웍스코리아 as the operator, at 6F Yongsan Station, 55 Hangang-daero 23-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul [5]. Note that a Japanese reseller listing calls Odense a "Danish tableware brand" [29]. This is wrong: only the name comes from Denmark [3][4].
- **From tableware line to lifestyle brand.** A Kolon Mall magazine article (undated, about 2023) lists LEGODT (레고트) as one of Odense's four lines. It describes LEGODT as a modular line with distinctive colors and calls it Odense's bestseller [3]. A LEGODT goblet (350ml, ceramic, 20,000 KRW) lists a release month of February 2022 [5]. 29CM introduces LEGODT as "a contemporary lifestyle brand newly developed by the ceramics brand Odense." It says the brand offers witty products that break away from existing lifestyles and help people forget everyday boredom [1][2]. 29CM ran a LEGODT showcase from October 9 to 22, 2023 [2].
- **Launch date.** No official launch date was found. The evidence points to 2022 for the tableware line and 2023 for the tumbler-led lifestyle brand. An Odense store notice about a new LEGODT store opening is dated August 10, 2023. Loop Tumbler listings show release dates of September 5, 2023 (Loop Tumbler) and December 1, 2023 (Two-way Loop Tumbler) [6][7][9]. See Open questions.
- **Who runs it.** In Korea, Brandworks Korea runs the brand [10][34]. In Japan, Manamana Co., Ltd. (株式会社マナマナ) runs the official Japanese store legodt.jp and the Japanese pop-ups. Manamana is based in Minami-Aoyama, Minato-ku, Tokyo, and its CEO is Makoto Kimizuka [21][25].

## Key findings

### Brand concept and message

- **TUMGGU (텀꾸 / タムク).** This is the brand's signature word, from "tumbler" plus "to decorate." legodt.jp calls it "the symbol of the joy and individuality of customizing your LEGODT tumbler" [21][25].
- **Taglines on legodt.jp:** "Dress Up Your Tumbler", "Make it pop. Make it you.", "What's Your Tumbler?", and "Stay in the Loop — New Vibes Ahead" [25].
- **Campaign themes:** "PLAY MY WAY" (Olive Young N Seongsu pop-up, 2025) [12][13]. "PLAYCOLOR! Mystyle! customizeForMe!" and "遊ぶように選ぶあなた色ストア" ("a store where you choose your own color as if playing") in Japan, 2025 [22]. "What's your pick today?" (PLUPPY pop-up, 2026) [18].
- **Brand voice in quotes.** A LEGODT representative said tumblers have moved "beyond the eco-friendly role of reducing disposables" to become "a fashion item that shows taste and personality" (2024) [11]. In 2026 the brand called the tumbler "a new lifestyle item that expresses personal taste and individuality." It said it hopes customers enjoy "the process of choosing, combining, and completing" [15].
- **Visual identity.** No brand guideline was found. Sources describe the look as colorful and pop, with cute silhouettes [21][22]. Color stories include milky tones [24], "vitamin" brights next to white and lavender [28], and neutral colors for office and gym use [23]. Named colors on legodt.jp: pink, mint, light gray, light blue, apple green, and butter cream [25]. Other named colors: Orange Carrot and Vanilla [31], Lavender [24], and Gradient Blue [16]. Bunny and heart keyrings are recurring motifs [11][25].

### Products

- **Loop Tumbler family.** The signature product. Its loop-shaped top handle is a key feature [28][35].
  - Loop Tumbler / Loop Tumbler Standard: 400ml and 600ml [24][28][31]. The 600ml has a stainless steel 304 body with a powder-coated outside, and comes with a body, lid, and sleeve [6].
  - Loop Tumbler Multi: 400ml, 600ml, and 1000ml [25]. It is the most popular type in Korea. You can sip from the lid or use a straw [24].
  - Two-way Loop Tumbler: 450ml and 720ml. It has a ceramic-coated stainless steel 304 body and a PP and rubber lid [7][8]. A review site says the ceramic coating reduces metallic smell. It also says the lid gasket cannot be removed, which makes cleaning harder [32].
  - Loop Tumbler (Tritan): 532ml, in 4 colors [36].
  - Loop Tumbler Module: 414ml (Pokémon edition) [16].
  - Loop Tumbler Handy 1000ml: seen only in a reseller search listing, not verified (see Open questions).
- **Modular tumbler (2026).** Launched in June 2026. Buyers choose and assemble the handle, lid, body, and sleeve from 28 parts. Earlier decorating was limited to stickers and keyrings [15].
- **Performance claims.** Insulation "comparable to a thermos," keeping drinks hot or cold for 6 hours. Also a spill-proof structure and an ergonomic handle [23]. The 2024 press describes "ergonomic shape and strong sealing" [11].
- **TUMGGU accessories.** Keyrings (for example, the TUMGGU Bunny Key Ring in brown and black), charms (for example, the TUMGGU Acrylic Ribbon, ¥1,000), stickers, straps, straws, and colored sleeves [10][11][24][25]. The 2024 limited "TUMGGU Kit" had a ceramic tumbler, bunny and heart keyrings, stickers, and a strap [11].
- **Beyond tumblers.** Ceramic tableware from the original line, such as goblets, plates, and cutlery sets [3][5]. Lunch boxes, a 24-inch suitcase, and pouch keyrings appear in the Pokémon collection [16]. Pop-ups also show travel items such as carriers [17].
- **Made in.** Tumbler listings show China as the country of manufacture [6][34].

### How customization works

1. **Pick a tumbler.** Choose the body, size, and color (Loop Tumbler line) [24][25].
2. **Swap the sleeve or parts.** Change the sleeve color [24]. With the modular tumbler, also pick the handle, lid, and body from 28 parts [15].
3. **Add charms and keyrings.** Attach TUMGGU keyrings and charms [11][24].
4. **Finish with stickers and straps.** The brand says keyrings, stickers, and straps allow more than 180 tumbler designs [11].
5. **Try it online first.** Since July 2025, a 3D configurator on the official website lets shoppers combine 5 tumbler body and lid styles with straps, keyrings, stickers, and straws. It shows real-size views, 360° rotation, and live prices, and links to checkout. It was built with the 3D startup Planingo [10][13].

At Japanese pop-ups, buying a tumbler plus one item lets shoppers make an original key charm. Every buyer gets free stickers [23][24].

### Channels

- **Korea, online:** the official LEGODT website, which has membership and a 3D configurator [10][18]. The Odense online store [5]. 29CM [1][16]. Musinsa [11]. Olive Young [30]. Kurly [34]. The Korean official URL could not be confirmed (see Open questions).
- **Korea, offline:** a new LEGODT store opening was announced in August 2023 [9]. Odense department-store counters (38 Odense locations as of 2022) [4]. Olive Young stores: Japanese press says LEGODT items sold out at major Olive Young stores [21][22].
- **Japan, online:** legodt.jp, run by Manamana. It ships from stock in Japan, offers Japanese support, and has passed Food Sanitation Act testing. Shipping is free on orders of ¥8,000 (tax included) or more. Contact: official@legodt.jp [25]. The legodt.jp FAQ, seen in a search listing, says there are no physical stores, only the online store and pop-ups [26]. Resellers such as BUYMA also sell LEGODT items [28][29].
- **Takashimaya:** no permanent counter was found. The only Takashimaya link found is a pop-up at PLAZA Tamagawa Takashimaya S.C. (South Building B1, Tokyo), from May 11 to June 14, 2026 [27].

### Target customers and popularity

- **Target.** Young shoppers in their 20s and 30s (MZ) who treat tumblers as fashion. The brand chose Olive Young N Seongsu as "a hot spot for foreigners and the MZ generation" [13][14]. Japanese press calls it popular with young people in Korea. The Japan pop-ups pitch decorating in "your favorite idol's color," linking the brand to K-pop fans [21][22].
- **Popularity signals.** About 1,500 visitors per day at the 2025 Olive Young N Seongsu pop-up [14]. Collaboration items reportedly sold out on the first day of Korean pop-ups, and items sold out at major Olive Young stores (brand claims repeated in Japanese press releases) [21][22]. Kolon Mall called the LEGODT line Odense's bestseller [3]. No sales figures for LEGODT alone were found. One industry report put Odense's yearly sales at around 30 billion KRW (see Open questions).

## Trends and outlook

- **From decoration to construction.** The brand has moved from stickers and keyrings (2024) to an online 3D configurator (2025) to a 28-part modular tumbler (2026) [10][11][15].
- **Pop-ups as the main stage.** Korean pop-ups grew from 2 in 2024 to several in 2026, often tied to collaborations (Pokémon, PLUPPY) [11][15][17][18].
- **Japan expansion.** Since July 2025, Manamana has run four or more Japanese pop-ups and the legodt.jp store, leaning on K-pop and "K-trend" demand [21][22][23][27].
- **Beyond tumblers.** Travel goods (suitcases, carriers) and lunch boxes suggest a wider lifestyle range [16][17].

- **Hero:** "Dress Up Your Tumbler." / "Make it pop. Make it you." [25]. Supporting line: TUMGGU, tumbler + decorate [25].
- **Features:** six-hour hot and cold retention, a spill-proof lid, an ergonomic loop handle, stainless steel 304, and sizes from 400ml to 1L [6][23][24][25].
- **How to customize:** 1. Pick your tumbler. 2. Choose sleeve and parts. 3. Add charms and keyrings. 4. Finish with stickers and straps. 5. Preview in 3D [10][11][15][24].
- **Lineup:** Loop Tumbler, Loop Tumbler Multi, Two-way Loop Tumbler, modular tumbler, and TUMGGU accessories [7][15][24][25].
- **Collaborations:** Pokémon, Mangom, PLUPPY [16][18][30].
- **Where to buy:** official websites (KR and legodt.jp), 29CM, Musinsa, Olive Young, Odense, and pop-ups (list upcoming dates) [1][11][25][30].
- **FAQ candidates:** Is there a physical store in Japan? (No, online and pop-ups only [26].) How long does it keep drinks hot or cold? (6 hours [23].) What is TUMGGU? [25]. What is it made of? (Stainless steel 304, some models ceramic-coated [6][7].) Is it dishwasher-safe? (Not confirmed.)
