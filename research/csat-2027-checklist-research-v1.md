# 2027 CSAT (Suneung, 19 November 2026): Exam-Day Checklist for Test Takers and Parents

- Date: 2026-10-31
- Scope: Practical exam-day facts for the 2027 academic-year College Scholastic Ability Test (2027학년도 대학수학능력시험, held on Thursday 19 November 2026): key dates, check-in time and timetable, what to bring, prohibited items, cheating rules and penalties, lost admission ticket, and government support relevant to parents (commute time, transport, noise control). It is written as source material for a Korean Naver blog post. It leaves out question content, difficulty, subject selection strategy, score interpretation, and university admissions. Each fact is labeled as **[Official]** (KICE, Ministry of Education, education office) or **[Secondary]** (news, private guides) where the difference matters.

## Summary

- The exam is on **Thursday 19 November 2026**. All test takers must be in their exam room by **08:10**. The building opens at **06:30**. The exam runs from **08:40 to 17:45** [2][9]. The pre-exam assembly (예비소집) is held by each provincial education office on the day before the exam, with the time and place printed on the application receipt [2]. Secondary sources give the date as Wednesday 18 November [15][16].
- **Bring:** admission ticket (수험표), an ID with a photo, and an analog watch with no payment, communication, or electronic display functions. The exam room provides black computer-use signature pens and 0.5 mm black mechanical pencils. Lunch and drinking water are **not** provided [2][4].
- **Do not bring any electronic device**, whether or not you plan to use it. This covers phones, smartwatches, smart glasses, tablets, earphones, digital-display watches, tumblers with displays, e-cigarettes, and power banks. Anything brought by mistake must be handed to the proctor **before Period 1 starts**. If it is not handed in, it counts as cheating [2][4].
- **Period 4 (Korean History plus inquiry subjects) is where most rule-based cheating cases happen.** During each 30-minute inquiry slot you may look only at that subject's booklet. Marking answers after the end bell, including filling in the self-scoring sheet, is cheating. Any cheating voids **the whole exam**, not only that period [4][12].
- **Key dates:** answer objections 19 to 23 November, final answers 1 December, score reports **Friday 11 December 2026** [1][12]. For parents, the government has asked offices and companies to start work **at 10:00 or later**, will run extra Seoul-area subway trains from 06:00 to 08:10, and will block cars from 200 m in front of exam sites [9][10].

## Background

The CSAT is administered by the Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation (KICE, 한국교육과정평가원) under the Ministry of Education (MOE, 교육부). The "2027 academic year" exam is held in calendar year 2026 and is used for university entry in 2027 [7][8]. KICE published the basic implementation plan (시행기본계획) on 31 March 2026 [7][8] and the detailed implementation plan (시행세부계획) on 1 July 2026 [5][6]. It then posted the 2027 test-taker guidelines (수험생 유의사항) on 15 October 2026 [3] and a cheating-prevention notice on 16 October 2026 [4]. On 20 October 2026 the MOE reported a cross-government support plan for exam day to the Cabinet [9][10].

The MOE expects 551,864 applicants (2,310 fewer than the year before) at 1,312 exam sites in 85 exam districts [9][10].

A new feature this year is a **self-scoring sheet (가채점표) printed on the back of the admission ticket**. Test takers may fill it in during exam time only [12][13][14].

## Key findings

### 1. Exam date, check-in time, and timetable

- **Exam date:** Thursday 19 November 2026 [1][7][8].
- **Check-in:** "All test takers must enter the exam room by 08:10. Entry is allowed from 06:30." [Official] [2]. Secondary sources add that from Period 2 onward you must be seated 10 minutes before each period starts [15].
- **Overall hours:** 08:40 to 17:45. Test takers with accommodations (including severe visual impairment) finish as late as 21:48 [Official] [9].
- **English listening:** 13:10 to 13:35. Aircraft schedules are adjusted for this window [Official] [9].
- **Period 4 structure:** Korean History (30 min) comes first, then the inquiry subjects. Each inquiry subject has 30 minutes, followed by a 2-minute booklet collection [Official] [2].
- **Full period-by-period timetable:** The official timetable is in the KICE notice attachments (HWP/PDF), which could not be opened for this note. The table in "Data and numbers" therefore comes from a secondary source [15]. It matches every official anchor point that could be checked: 08:10 check-in, 08:40 start, English listening from 13:10, 30-minute Korean History and inquiry slots with a 2-minute collection, and a 17:45 finish [2][9].

### 2. What to bring, what is provided, and what is allowed

**Must bring [Official]:** the admission ticket and a resident registration card or another ID that proves who you are [2].

**Provided in the exam room [Official]:** black computer-use signature pens and 0.5 mm black mechanical pencils are handed out to everyone. The proctor holds white correction tape and spare 0.5 mm leads and gives them out on request [2]. You may still bring your own signature pen, but only one without any non-permitted function. KICE gives the example of a pen with a red tip as not allowed [12][13].

**Allowed during the exam [Official] [2][4]:** ID, admission ticket, black computer-use signature pen, white correction tape, black pencil, eraser, black 0.5 mm mechanical pencil lead, and an analog watch with hour and minute hands that has **no** payment, communication (including Bluetooth), or LCD/LED display function. A mask is also allowed after the proctor checks it.
- Writing tools other than the black pencil, black computer-use signature pen, and mechanical pencil are not allowed [4].
- If it is unclear whether an item is allowed, the proctor checks it each period [4].
- Items needed for a physical condition or medical reason (for example, a magnifier, earplugs, or a cushion) may be used after the proctor inspects them each period [2][4].
- There are no clocks in exam rooms, so bring your own watch [2]. In Periods 1 and 3, proctors set aside time before the exam starts to check IDs and watches [2].

**Not provided [Official]:** lunch and drinking water [2]. Bring your own lunch box and water.

**Accepted IDs: details not confirmed from an official source.** KICE says only "a resident registration card or another ID that proves who you are" and "an ID with a photo" [2]. Secondary guides list the resident registration card, driver's licence, a valid passport, and a youth ID card. They say a student ID counts only if it shows a photo, name, date of birth, and the principal's seal, and that **mobile IDs are not accepted** [15][16].

### 3. Prohibited items and cheating

**Items you cannot bring into the exam site [Official] [2][4]:** "As a rule, every electronic device is banned from the exam site, regardless of its function or whether it would be used during the exam." This covers:
- Anything with communication (including Bluetooth) or payment functions: phones, smartwatches, smart glasses, tablets, earphones.
- Anything with an electronic display (LCD, LED): electronic dictionaries, tumblers with a display, digital watches.
- Any rechargeable item: e-cigarettes, power banks.

**Before Period 1 [Official]:** If you brought a banned item by mistake, hand it to the proctor before Period 1 starts. If you do not, you are treated as having cheated [2][4].

**Items allowed in the building but not during the exam [Official] [4]:** before each period, put them in your bag and leave them at the front or in the hallway. Some items are confiscated if found during the exam: tracing paper, practice paper, a personal mechanical pencil, a highlighter, or a ballpoint pen. Others are treated as cheating at once if found: textbooks, reference books, or past exam papers.

**Period 4 procedure for test takers who chose two inquiry subjects [Official] [4]:**
1. Copy the handwriting-check phrase from the Period 4 inquiry booklet cover into the answer sheet.
2. Take the first-choice and second-choice booklets out of the bundle.
3. Write your name, exam number, and subject order (1 or 2) at the top of each booklet.
4. Put the first-choice booklet on the desk. Fold the remaining booklets (other subjects plus the second-choice one) in half and put them in the booklet envelope on the floor, together with the second-choice booklet.
5. Wait quietly with the answer sheet on the first-choice booklet until the bell rings.
- Korean History and inquiry use separate answer sheets [4][12].

**Period 4 cheating types [Official] [4]:**
- Writing or changing answers for a subject after its end bell has rung, **including the self-scoring sheet**. Examples: changing first-choice answers during the second-choice slot, or marking answers during the booklet change.
- During a 30-minute slot, looking at any subject's booklet other than the one for that slot, or having both of your chosen subjects' booklets out at once. Examples: looking at the second-choice booklet during the first-choice slot, or doing either of these during the booklet change.
- KICE notes that marking answers after the end bell has been reported by other test takers and treated as cheating in past years [4].

**General rule [Official]:** Self-scoring is allowed only during exam time. Calculating your score after the end bell counts as cheating, and you must put down your writing tools when the bell rings [12].

**Penalties:**
- [Official] "If treated as cheating, not only that period but **the entire exam is void**." [4]
- [Official] Korean History is mandatory. Skipping it voids the whole exam, and no score report is issued [8][12].
- [Secondary] Under Article 34 of the Higher Education Act (고등교육법), the exam is void, and for intentional or serious cheating the test taker is also **barred from the exam for one year** [17]. The current text of the law was not checked directly for this note (see Open questions).

### 5. Practical tips: the day before and the exam day

**The day before (pre-exam assembly):**
- [Official] Attend the pre-exam assembly to get your admission ticket. Check your name, exam number, and chosen subjects against your application [2][16].
- [Secondary] Find the exam school and its entrances in advance. On assembly day you can usually visit the school, but you cannot enter the building with the exam rooms [16].
- [Official] Check the weather for your exam site on the Korea Meteorological Administration website (available from 13 November) [9].
- [Practical] Pack the night before: admission ticket, ID, analog watch, lunch, and water. Leave your phone at home or plan to hand it in before Period 1 [2][4].

**Lost admission ticket:**
- [Official] Get a reissued ticket **before check-in time** so that it does not affect your exam [2].
- [Secondary] Bring one print of the same photo used on your application, plus your ID, to the exam site's management office (시험장 관리본부) **by 08:00** [16]. See Conflicts below about the deadline.

**On the exam day:**
- [Official] Arrive by 08:10. The building opens at 06:30 [2].
- [Official] Cars are blocked from 200 m in front of exam sites, so drivers must drop test takers off before that point and let them walk [9][10].
- [Official] Hand in any electronic device before Period 1 [2][4].
- [Official] Fill in the self-scoring sheet on the back of the admission ticket only during exam time [12][13].

**Lunch and health:** KICE confirms only that lunch and water are not provided [2]. Advice on what to eat or how to manage your condition is not covered by official sources and is not included here.

### 6. Parent-relevant information (MOE support plan, 20 Oct 2026) [Official] [9][10]

- **Work hours:** Government offices and companies are asked to start work **at 10:00 or later** on exam day. This is a request, not a requirement.
- **Transport:** Extra Seoul-area subway trains will run from **06:00 to 08:10**. Emergency vehicles from police stations and other public bodies will be placed along routes test takers use. Each province will arrange transport for test takers on islands and in remote areas, and will prepare snow-removal measures.
- **Car access:** Cars are blocked **from 200 m in front of** exam sites.
- **Noise:** Aircraft and helicopter takeoffs and landings are adjusted, and military training such as artillery fire and tank movements is paused, during English listening (**13:10 to 13:35**). Outside that window, buses and trains near exam sites are asked to slow down and avoid horns, and events and construction sites are asked to keep noise down.
- **Safety:** Education offices in all 16 provinces inspect every exam site. The government is preparing an earthquake response and information system.

## Trends and outlook

- **New this year:** the self-scoring sheet is printed on the back of the admission ticket [12][13][14]. This makes it easier to self-score legally during exam time. KICE's cheating notice stresses that writing on it after the end bell is cheating [4].
- **The rules for electronic devices and Period 4 stay strict.** KICE describes them as the main risk areas, and a violation voids the entire exam [4].
- **Subject structure:** Korean, Math, and vocational inquiry use a common-plus-elective format. Social and science inquiry allow up to two of 17 subjects. English, Korean History, and second foreign language / Chinese characters are graded on an absolute scale [7][8]. This note does not cover subject strategy.

## Open questions

**Not confirmed from an official source in this note:**
1. **Full period-by-period timetable.** The official timetable is in the KICE notice attachments (HWP/PDF), which could not be read. The table above comes from a secondary source [15]. Every official anchor point that could be checked matches it, but readers should check it against the KICE guidelines PDF [3] before publishing.
2. **Exact pre-exam assembly date and time.** KICE says only "the day before the exam" (so 18 November follows), with the time and place on the application receipt [2]. The exact hours vary by education office [16].
3. **List of accepted IDs and whether mobile IDs are accepted.** These details come only from secondary guides [15][16].
4. **One-year suspension under Article 34 of the Higher Education Act.** This comes from a 2024 news report [17]. The current text of the law was not checked directly.
5. **Score report times (09:00, 14:00, 18:00) and the fee refund period (23 to 27 November).** These come from a news report that summarizes the detailed plan [12], not from the plan itself.
6. **Rules for test takers who chose only one inquiry subject** (for example, where to wait during the first slot). These were not found in readable official text.
7. **Practical health and lunch advice.** No official source was found beyond "lunch and water are not provided" [2].

**Conflicts between sources:**
- **Deadline for reissuing a lost admission ticket:** KICE says "before check-in time" (08:10) [2]. A secondary guide quotes a KICE Q&A as "report to the management office by 08:00" [16]. Advise readers to go to the management office as early as possible, and no later than 08:00.
- **Publication date of the detailed plan:** a district education office notice from 31 March 2026 gives 6 July 2026 (Monday), as the basic plan scheduled it [11]. KICE and MOE actually published it on 1 July 2026 [1][5][6]. The MOE web page shows a posting date of 30 June, but its attachment is a press release for the morning papers of 1 July [6]. Use 1 July.
- **Year labels:** "2027학년도" refers to the exam held in November **2026**. Some automated summaries and older articles mix up the years, so check the year whenever a source mentions "2027".
